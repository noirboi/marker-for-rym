Ever browse RateYourMusic charts, lists, or discographies and wonder which albums or movies you've already rated? Marker for RYM is a lightweight extension that highlights your personal ratings directly on the page—so you never have to guess again.

Available for Chrome and Firefox.

📋 What’s new in 1.2.0
• Firefox support (same extension package; Firefox 140+)
• Fixed whole-page green highlight on “releases added” / contributed pages
• Fixed green padding around list cover images when the cover is a rated release
• No more duplicate ★ badges on director filmography pages (/films/…) where RYM already shows your score
• Stopped highlighting large text blocks (e.g. artist Biography) and review-page chrome
• Fixed over-highlighting in list “Edit item” UI
• Branding refresh: new name, logo, icons, and popup polish
• Link to the RYM forum thread for news, feedback, and support

Say goodbye to opening multiple tabs just to check your own scores. Soft row highlights and optional color-coded star badges make your music and film collection visible as you explore the site.

✨ Key Features:

🎨 Color-Coded Badges: Optional ★ labels with a clean, solid color system by rating tier (can be turned off in the popup; row highlights stay on).

⚡ Lightning Fast & Local: Your ratings are synced and stored in your browser’s local storage. Zero server delays, no unnecessary load on RYM, and badges/highlights appear as you browse.

🔄 Smart One-Click Sync: Open the popup and click “Sync Ratings” to pull your latest music and film scores. Sync runs in your open RYM tab and handles large libraries smoothly.

🎯 Clutter-Free UI: Highlights and badges appear on charts, custom lists, and similar pages—and stay out of the way on your own profile/collection and on artist/director pages where RYM already shows your score.

🛠️ How to Use
1. Install the extension and pin it to your browser toolbar.
2. Make sure you are logged into your RateYourMusic account.
3. Open the Marker for RYM popup and click “Sync Ratings”.
4. Browse any chart, list, or discography and see your rated items marked.
(Tip: Use “Force Full Sync” if you’ve recently deleted ratings or want a completely fresh scan.)

🔒 Privacy First
Marker for RYM is free, non-commercial, and respects your privacy. It only uses your logged-in RYM session to sync ratings for local display. No personal data is collected, sold, or sent to third-party servers.
